# Dayarc Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Dayarc is a Chrome extension that counts the time since the last occurrence of a habit you choose. This policy explains what happens to your information.

## The short version

Dayarc does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your own device.

## What Dayarc stores

Only what you type into it:

- Habit names
- The date and time you enter for each habit
- An optional cost per day
- Your streak history (longest streak and number of restarts)
- A display setting (whether to hide habit names)

This information is saved in your browser using Chrome's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`). It is never sent to the developer or to any server.

## What Dayarc does not do

- No accounts or sign-in
- No analytics, tracking, advertising, or cookies
- No network requests of any kind
- No remote code
- No access to the websites you visit or your browsing history
- No sale or transfer of data to third parties

## Sensitive information

Habit names you enter may reveal personal information, for example a health-related habit. This information stays on your device. You can turn on "Hide habit names in the menu" so names are not displayed, and you can delete any habit at any time.

## Backups you create

The "Export backup" button saves a file to your computer. That file is created by you, stays where you save it, and contains the habits you entered. Keep it private. The developer cannot see it.

## Deleting your data

Delete a habit inside the extension to remove it. Removing the extension from Chrome deletes all of its stored data from your browser.

## Children

Dayarc is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Not medical advice

Dayarc is a personal counter. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and it is not a substitute for professional care.

## Limited Use

Dayarc's use of information adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is used only to provide the extension's single purpose, is never transferred to anyone, is never used for advertising, and is never read by humans.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date. Any change that involves collecting data would require an update to the extension listing as well.
